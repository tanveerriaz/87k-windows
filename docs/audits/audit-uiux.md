# 87K Windows — UI/UX & Product Design Audit

**Method.** The five committed captures in `assets/video/captures/` predate commit `d87820c` ("reframe 87K Windows around mutual listening"), which rewrote `landing-page.tsx`, added 179 lines to `join-page.tsx` and 124 to `global.css`. They no longer show the product. So I booted the app (`INFERENCE_PROVIDER=mock GEMINI_FACILITATOR=mock`, ports 15173/13001), drove the full storyteller + listener + wall flow with Playwright, and captured/measured the **current** build at the real target sizes — 390×844 (senior's phone) and 1280×720 (projected wall). Computed styles and WCAG ratios below are measured, not estimated. Captures and measurements: `scratchpad/shots/`.

## Summary

The emotional core is strong: amber windows in a dark façade, an honest no-match, provenance written in a senior's language. But the build regressed against its own QA. At 1280×720 the landing page overflows 229px — the "I would like to listen" action is 60% clipped, the privacy promise falls below the fold — and a 320px façade strip collides with the headline. On the wall, Gemini's questions render at 13px, evidence chips at 9px, and `max-height: 300px` silently truncates the provenance disclosure. The primary consent button fails AA at 3.10:1.

## Top 3 mandated changes

**1. Make the wall survive projection — raise the floor to 18px and delete the silent clip. (S)**
Remove `max-height: 300px` (`global.css:1434`) and let the panel size to content, or move overflow into a paged reveal. Then raise the wall's type floor: evidence chips 9px → 20px (`:1509`), Gemini's questions 13px → 22px (`:1526`), journey rail 12px → 16px (`:1143`). Buy the space by giving the panel back to the façade — target ~55/45 façade-to-panel, restoring the two-window climax as the dominant image. Nothing else on the list changes what a judge across the room actually experiences.

**2. Fix the landing page's first 720 pixels. (S)**
Change `.two-chairs-page::before` to `background-size: cover` (or a full-bleed gradient-masked band) so the façade stops rendering as a floating rectangle across the headline (`global.css:1976-1978`). Then compress the hero so both role choices *and* the "No contact details are shared / Both people choose yes" assurance clear the fold at 1280×720 — tighten the H1 clamp and the video's `min-height`, currently 949px total against a 720px viewport. Swap `autoPlay` for poster-first click-to-play so the hero frame is chosen, not accidental. While in the file, restore the descriptive alt text and repair `demo.spec.ts:19`.

**3. Make consent as granular and as audible as the copy claims. (M)**
Three changes on `join-page.tsx`'s review stage, in priority order: (a) add a "Read this to me" button that speaks the summary, every capsule field, and the uncertainty list — reuse `speakGuide`'s `rate: 0.82 / en-SG` config from `:212-233`; (b) make each `PLACE / ERA / SKILL / OFFER / WANTS` chip removable with a 48px "Remove" affordance, so "You decide what enters matching" (`:422`) becomes literally true; (c) raise the chip labels from 9px to 16px, open the uncertainty `<details>` by default, and darken the CTA fill until cream text clears 4.5:1 (`#c86c43` → roughly `#9e4f2c`) — or take the label to 24px, which is the better answer for this audience anyway.

## One thing this design does better than the standard approach

**The no-match state leaves the sharer's window lit.**

The default pattern for "we found nothing" is an empty state: a grey illustration, a zero-results line, a "try again" button — the interface withdrawing the thing you asked for. Here (`05-wall-no-match-one-light-1920x1080.png`) the projected façade keeps **one amber window burning** in a wall of dark ones, headed `NO MATCH YET` with "We haven't found the right listener yet. No invitation was created, and the approved story remains safe."

Three things happen at once that the standard pattern cannot do. The visual says *you were heard* while the text says *no one matched* — so the absence of a listener never reads as an absence of worth. "YET" and "the right listener" locate the gap in the room's supply, not in the person who spoke. And because the wall renders exactly one light when one life was shared and two lights plus a thread only when evidence clears the threshold, **the honest outcome and the celebratory outcome are the same visual language at different intensities** — refusal is not a degraded state of the design, it is the design running correctly. Most consent-and-matching products bury the refusal path in a toast. This one projects it at 60px on the wall and makes it beautiful. That is the single strongest design decision in the repo, and it is worth protecting through every change above.
