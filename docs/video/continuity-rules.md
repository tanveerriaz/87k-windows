# Visual, ethical and editorial continuity

## One photographic world

- The building is an original fictional Singapore public-housing façade, not an exact real block.
- Use straight-on or near-straight-on architectural photography. Preserve the same façade geometry, courtyard level, window spacing and two hero-window positions across scenes.
- Palette: carbon black, damp concrete grey, tungsten amber, moon-blue and soft bone. Heritage teal is not a dominant field.
- Atmosphere is humid and still. Motion is limited to soft haze, a trace of foliage and controlled light changes.
- Never show identifiable residents, faces, names, unit numbers, addresses, logos, signs or readable private writing.
- Memory is warm and active. Avoid sepia mourning, dust motes as shorthand for death, abandoned rooms, wilted flowers, clocks counting down or melancholy portraits.

## Hero light grammar

- Before approval: no participant light.
- After approval: one amber window rises over about one second and remains on because the story has been witnessed.
- During matching: the first window may breathe by no more than 3% luminance. Do not flicker.
- Match: the second amber window rises, steadies, then one fine blue-white pulse travels between the windows.
- No match: the first amber window remains; no second light, line, pulse or invitation appears.
- Connection light is informational, not magical. No sparks, particles, beams into the sky or network-map effects.

## Model and product truth

- Local Gemma converts the submitted fictional memory into a reviewable safe capsule. It does not select a match or create the Gemini guide.
- The participant reviews and approves what may enter matching.
- Deterministic retrieval and scoring decides `MATCH` or `NO MATCH YET` from visible evidence.
- Gemini receives only two approved safe capsules, the evidence path and match explanation after `MATCH`. It prepares exactly two optional questions and a pause/stop reminder.
- Gemini never sees raw memory text, images, contact details or unmatched submissions.
- `NO MATCH YET` creates no invitation and never invokes Gemini.
- Generated footage must never invent interface states, scores, evidence, providers, acceptance or contact exchange.

## Typography and compositing

- Add all readable words in editorial. Never rely on generated text.
- Use at most two type families and preserve the app's accessible scale.
- Keep evidence labels verbatim: `QUEENSTOWN`, `1970s`, `RADIO REPAIR`, `TEACH <-> LEARN`.
- Keep `NO MATCH YET` in soft bone and amber, not warning red.
- Preserve opaque, high-contrast UI surfaces over photographic backgrounds.
- Avoid glassmorphism, teal gradients, decorative SVG façades and fake chat bubbles.

## Camera and transitions

- Architectural plates are locked or use a nearly imperceptible push of no more than 3% over eight seconds.
- Object inserts may use shallow depth of field; UI recordings stay optically flat and crisp.
- Use match dissolves between rectangular object highlights, windows and interface panels.
- Do not use whip pans, drone moves, speed ramps, glitch transitions or generative morphing of the building.
- Cut to actual UI for every claim; cut back to the façade for emotional consequence.

## Sound

- Base layer: restrained Singapore evening room tone without intelligible conversation.
- Object layer: quiet radio tuning texture and ceramic contact, used sparingly.
- Light layer: soft tungsten rise; one delicate connection tone for the traveling pulse.
- No generated speech, sentimental solo piano, heartbeat, alarm, notification chimes or synthetic companion voice.
- The `NO MATCH YET` scene resolves to calm room tone rather than silence or failure sound.

## Render review checklist

- [ ] Fictional and non-identifying.
- [ ] Architecture remains stable frame to frame.
- [ ] No faces, silhouettes, addresses, logos or readable generated writing.
- [ ] Window count and state match the scene grammar.
- [ ] No fake application UI.
- [ ] No unrequested camera motion or sci-fi effects.
- [ ] Colour and exposure match the shared façade world.
- [ ] Output file, prompt, model, time and SHA-256 are recorded.
- [ ] A human reviewer marked the asset approved before use.
